FAQ

Common questions

How much will you pay for my house?

Every house is different. We look at condition, location, recent comparable sales and the repairs needed, then make a fair cash offer and walk you through the math.

Is there any obligation to accept?

No. The offer is free and there's no pressure. If it doesn't work for you, that's fine.

How fast can you close?

Often in as little as 7 days. If you need more time to move, we'll set the date around your schedule.

Do I need to make repairs or clean out the house?

No. We buy as-is. Take what you want and leave the rest.

Will I pay any fees or commissions?

No. There are no commissions, and we pay the standard closing costs.

What if the house has tenants?

We buy properties with tenants in place. We'll talk through the lease and your options.

What if I inherited the house?

We work with inherited and probate properties regularly and can coordinate with your attorney or the estate.

What areas do you buy in?

Chicago and the surrounding suburbs in Cook and the collar counties. If you're not sure, just ask.
