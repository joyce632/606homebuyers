FAQ

Questions sellers ask us

Don't see yours? Call (773) 570-2800 and ask us directly.

How much will you pay for my house?

Every house is different. We look at condition, location, recent comparable sales and the repairs needed, then make a fair cash offer and walk you through the math.

How is this different from listing with a realtor?

There are no showings, repairs, commissions or long waits. You sell as-is and choose a closing date that works for you.

Is there any obligation to accept your offer?

No. Requesting an offer is free and there's no pressure to move forward.

How quickly can you close?

Often in as little as 7 days. If you need more time, we'll work around your schedule.

Do I need to make repairs or clean the house?

No. We buy as-is. You don't need to repair, clean out or prepare the house for showings.

Will I pay any fees or commissions?

No. There are no commissions, and we don't charge fees to make an offer.

What if my house has tenants?

We buy properties with tenants in place. We'll talk through the situation and your options.

What if I inherited the house?

We regularly work with inherited houses and estates and can coordinate with your attorney.

What types of houses do you buy?

Houses that need repairs, inherited homes, rentals, vacant houses, and homes where the owner simply wants a faster sale.
